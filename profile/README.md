**Student-run quantitative research group focused on probability, markets, and systematic decision-making.**

Chambers Street Research develops quantitative frameworks to understand and act on market behavior across prediction-driven and data-rich environments.

---

### History

Founded in **2025 in New York, New York**, Chambers Street Research began as a small group of students seeking a more applied and quantitative approach to understanding markets.

With early support from the **Westbury United Methodist Church**, the group established its initial structure and began developing its research and educational framework.

Today, Chambers Street Research is a multi-strategy research group focused on **probability-based analysis, prediction markets, quantitative research, and systematic thinking**.

### Mission

Our mission is to provide hands-on experience in:

* Quantitative research
* Strategy development
* Portfolio construction and risk
* Probability and statistical analysis
* Systematic decision-making

We aim to equip members with the technical and analytical skills needed to navigate modern financial markets while fostering **disciplined thinking, independent problem-solving, and a deeper understanding of markets under uncertainty.**

---

## Research

### Prediction Markets

We study short- to mid-term opportunities in prediction markets, focusing on:

* Market mispricing
* Information flow
* Event-based probability
* Forecasting
* Market efficiency

### Quantitative Research

Members develop and test research frameworks using:

* Statistics
* Probability
* Data analysis
* Market data
* Quantitative modeling

The goal is to identify, evaluate, and rigorously test potential trading opportunities.

### Strategy Development

We emphasize systematic approaches to research and decision-making through:

* Hypothesis development
* Backtesting
* Simulation
* Performance evaluation
* Iterative strategy refinement

### Portfolio Thinking

Research is evaluated through the lens of:

* Risk and return
* Diversification
* Correlation
* Position sizing
* Uncorrelated opportunity sets

## Education

### Foundational Training

At the beginning of each semester, members are introduced to:

* Probability
* Statistics
* Market structure
* Quantitative research
* Research methodology

### Applied Learning

Members gain hands-on experience through research projects, strategy testing, data analysis, and collaborative research.

### Advanced Topics

Depending on research interests, topics may include:

* Event-driven trading
* Prediction markets
* Forecasting
* Derivatives
* Market microstructure
* Quantitative risk
* Systematic strategies

### Skill Development

Beyond technical knowledge, we emphasize:

* Clear communication
* Independent research
* Intellectual curiosity
* Discipline
* Critical thinking
* Decision-making under uncertainty
